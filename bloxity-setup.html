Description
Math Tower Race is a multiplayer math battle. Vote for Easy, Medium, Hard or the secret Algebra mode, then race everyone in the server: every round a math question pops up on the big screen, and the faster you answer, the more blocks you stack on your tower. After each question the lava rises, and anyone whose tower is under it is out. The questions get harder every round: addition and times tables, sequences, shapes, areas and perimeters, angles, squares and square roots, percentages, fractions, order of operations and real algebra. Hints and second chances help you learn as you play. The last tower standing wins! Earn Gems for every right answer and every win, then spend them on tower skins, block animations, dances and cute pets that multiply your Gems (no in-app purchases: everything is earned with Gems). Climb the Top Wins and Top Answers leaderboards and become the math champion!

Hosting steps

  1. hosting.bloxity.io: create the app math-tower-race and copy its deploy token.
  2. GitHub: an empty public repo asivixlp44-cmyk/math-tower-race with the secret LEGION_DEPLOY_TOKEN, then push main (the workflow builds and deploys).

  4. Check https://math-tower-race.host.bloxity.io/health and play at https://math-tower-race.play.bloxity.io.
